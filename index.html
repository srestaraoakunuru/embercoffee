Our Story

Born from a Love of
the Perfect Roast

In the heart of the city, Ember was born from a simple belief: that extraordinary coffee comes from an unyielding devotion to craft. Every bean is hand-selected from sustainable farms across Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala, then roasted in small batches in our on-site roastery.

Our baristas aren't just skilled — they're storytellers, turning each cup into a narrative of origin, process, and passion.
